# sample_events.txt -- a scripted walk-through of every event type.
# Prices are integer ticks (like real exchanges).  Order ids are
# assigned 1, 2, 3, ... in the order the events arrive.

# --- build up both sides of the book (none of these cross) ---
LIMIT SELL 5001 12
LIMIT SELL 5002 8
LIMIT SELL 5003 5
LIMIT BUY 4999 9
LIMIT BUY 4998 20
LIMIT BUY 4997 15
BOOK

# --- two orders at the SAME price: time priority demo ---
# order #7 rests at 5001 BEHIND order #1 (which arrived first)
LIMIT SELL 5001 4
BOOK

# --- a market buy sweeps the ask side ---
# 14 contracts: fills all 12 of #1, then 2 of #7 (partial fill).
# #1 filled before #7 = time priority in action.
MARKET BUY 14
BOOK

# --- a crossing limit order ---
# willing to sell at 4998, but the best bid is 4999 -- so it trades
# at 4999 first (price improvement), then eats into 4998, and the
# leftover would rest... let's size it to rest 3 at 4998.
LIMIT SELL 4998 32
BOOK

# --- cancel a resting order ---
# order #6 was LIMIT BUY 15 @ 4997; cancel it by side + price + id
CANCEL BUY 4997 6
BOOK

# --- cancel that should FAIL (order already fully filled) ---
CANCEL SELL 5001 1
BOOK 10
